The berries, redrawn

The same six characters, drawn more like their berries, and a bit cuter. The shape on its own should say which berry it is, even at 18px in a tab. Texture shows only at sizes where it can be seen, and the face stays the one you know, with a small mouth added. Texture fades to a third around the face, so it doesn't crowd it.

01Each berry, now and redrawn.

Each has a light shine and a little shade underneath. At 36px they lose their texture and at 18px their mouth too, so a tab icon stays clean.
