AShelf: a catalogue, and a product page

Like a well-made shop: every mascot on the same quiet grey stage, name and adds under it, nothing else. Pointing at one shows Add on its stage. Opening one is a page of its own, not a list beside it: a big stage on the left with the mascot live (its moods as a small switch), what it’s told under it, and a right column with the name, Add, and its facts as rows. Back is the crumb and the toolbar’s Back.

What it’s told word for word, as the model reads it
You watch prices for the user. When they name a product, find it on prisjakt.nu and note the lowest price and which shop has it. Each morning, look again. If the lowest price has dropped by 5% or more since you last looked, say so in one line: the product, the new price, the old price and the shop. If nothing dropped, say nothing. Never buy anything, never sign in, and never fill in a form.
