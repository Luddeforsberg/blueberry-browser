Blueberry

An AI browser that works on your behalf

Grow a flock of birds for the jobs you repeat. Blueberry notices what you do and offers to take it over.

    • Grow a flock. Make a bird for a job you repeat, with a name, a look, its own instructions, and limits on which sites it may visit and whether it may click or type.
    • It asks first. Anything that would change something waits on a card that says exactly what will happen, and nothing goes until you say yes.
    • It works behind you. The tabs a bird opens stay behind yours. Watch it work, or click in and take over.
    • Share a bird. Publish one to the Gallery and send its link. Anyone can read what it’s told, word for word, before adding their own copy.

    Getting it

    Blueberry isn’t out for download yet. If someone sent you a mascot’s link, that page shows everything it would be told and allowed to do.
